Modernised the deployment and infrastructure for a government digital services platform spanning AWS and Azure.

- **50%+ reduction in deployment effort** by rebuilding CI/CD pipelines with Terraform and Helm, replacing manual runbooks with automated, repeatable infrastructure.
- **Security-first CI/CD** — integrated automated security scanning (dependency checks, container image scanning, secret detection) into every pipeline stage, meeting GDS compliance requirements without slowing releases.
- **Mentored 4 junior engineers** on cloud-native patterns, infrastructure-as-code, and secure coding practices. Two were promoted within the year.
